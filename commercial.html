How we work

What a professional job includes

  • Preparation is most of the job.

    On steel and masonry that means washing, treating rust, feathering failed coatings, and the right primer for the surface — the same rule as houses: the coating only holds if what's under it is sound. Preparation decides whether the finish is still holding in ten years, and it's the first thing a low bid cuts.

  • Coats are determined by the job.

    Some jobs need two coats; some need four. It depends on the color change, the surface, and the product. We apply what the job requires — not a number picked to win a bid.

  • Your property is protected.

    We cover floors, mask what isn't being painted, move and protect furniture, and keep the site orderly for the duration of the work.

  • Final walkthrough.

    Abe walks every completed job and inspects it himself before we call it finished.

  • Cleanup is part of the job.

    The site is left clean and ready to use.

If the job turns out bigger than the photos showed

If a job needs significantly more scraping or repair than what we could see when we quoted it, that's a separate charge — but we tell you before we do it, in writing, and you decide. Same rules as our photo estimates: no work happens on something you didn't agree to, and no surprise line items at the end.
