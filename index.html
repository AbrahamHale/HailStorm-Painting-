How I work

What separates me from a guy with a roller

  • Prep is most of the job.

    Scraping, caulking, masking, sanding, priming bare spots. It's where the time goes, and it decides whether the paint is still holding in ten years. It's also the first thing a cheap bid cuts.

  • Coats: what the job needs.

    Some jobs take two coats. Some take four. It depends on the color change, the surface, and the product — not on a number picked to win a bid.

  • I take care of the house.

    Covering, masking, moving and protecting furniture, keeping the site clean while I work.

  • I check it before I call it done.

    I walk the job and go over it before I tell you it's finished.

  • I clean up.

    You get your house back.
