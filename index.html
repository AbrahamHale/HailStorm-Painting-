The renewal in the name

Old surfaces, brought back

Renewal means bringing old surfaces back — cabinets, log homes, decks, trim, and furniture. Refinishing work, not disaster work.

To be clear about the boundary: we don't do water, fire, or smoke damage repair, mold remediation, or flood cleanup — that's a different trade. We do finishes. If a surface is worn, faded, chalking, or just dated, that's the work we're built for.

How we work

What a professional job includes

  • Preparation is most of the job.

    Scraping, caulking, masking, sanding, and priming bare spots. Preparation decides whether the paint is still holding in ten years, and it's the first thing a low bid cuts.

  • Coats are determined by the job.

    Some jobs need two coats; some need four. It depends on the color change, the surface, and the product — not on a number picked to win a bid.

  • Your property is protected.

    We cover floors, mask what isn't being painted, move and protect furniture, and keep the site orderly for the duration of the work.

  • Final walkthrough.

    Abe walks every completed job and inspects it himself before we call it finished.

  • Cleanup is part of the job.

    The site is left clean and ready to use.
