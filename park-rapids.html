Painting in Park Rapids, Minnesota

I'm Abe Hale, owner of Heritage Painting in nearby Nimrod. Park Rapids is part of my regular service area — call and I'll come take a look, or send photos and you'll have a written price without waiting on a site visit.

Lakes country work

Around Park Rapids a lot of my calls are lake places: cabins, decks that face the water and take the weather, and log homes due for stain. That's wood work as much as paint work — knowing when a deck needs a wash and a coat versus a strip and sand, and what a log wall needs to last another ten years. It's a large part of my work.
