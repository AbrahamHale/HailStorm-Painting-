About

Ten-plus years in the trade. One name on the work.

I grew up in Alaska — hunting, fishing, hiking, and working with my hands. I came up in construction there, traveling to jobs in remote towns and villages, some you could only reach by plane: commercial buildings, houses, fire halls, whatever the community needed built or fixed. Different country, different weather, different problems on every job.

That kind of work teaches you to get it right the first time — there's no running to the store when the store is a flight away. Construction taught me the trades. Painting is the one I got good at, and it's what I've done for more than ten years now, in Alaska and Minnesota.

These days I run Heritage Painting & Renewal out of Nimrod and work throughout Wadena, Hubbard, Todd, and Cass counties. It's a one-person company by design: I estimate the job, I do the work, and I answer the phone. I like the work and I'm good at it.

I don't call a job done until I've walked it and checked it myself. And what I tell you I'll do is what I'll do.

My guarantee

I stand behind my work. If something I did isn't right, I come back and fix it.

The renewal in the name

Old surfaces, brought back

Renewal means bringing old surfaces back — cabinets, log homes, decks, trim, and furniture. Refinishing work, not disaster work.

I don't do water, fire, or smoke damage repair, mold remediation, or flood cleanup — that's a different trade. I do finishes: the surfaces you see and touch, made to look right again.
