About us

About Heritage Painting & Renewal

Heritage Painting & Renewal is a painting and refinishing company based in Nimrod, Minnesota. We paint and refinish homes and commercial buildings across Wadena, Hubbard, Todd, and Cass counties — interiors, exteriors, cabinets, decks, fences, and log homes.

The company is built on more than ten years of professional painting, with roots in commercial and residential construction across remote Alaska — including villages and job sites reachable only by air. On work like that, there is no second trip to the store and no one coming behind to fix things. It teaches planning, preparation, and getting the job right the first time. Those habits are the company's standards today.

Three things carry every job we take: preparation, honest scope, and work that lasts. Preparation, because paint only holds as long as what's under it. Honest scope, because a written price should say what's included — the prep, the products, the coats — and change only in writing, with your approval. And work that lasts, because painting a job twice costs more than doing it right once.

We serve homeowners and businesses alike: houses, cabins, and lake places on one side; storefronts, churches, shops, halls, and municipal buildings on the other. Every job is inspected before we call it complete.

Our guarantee

We stand behind our work. If something isn't right, we come back and fix it.

The renewal in the name

Old surfaces, brought back

Renewal means bringing old surfaces back — cabinets, log homes, decks, trim, and furniture. Refinishing work, not disaster work.

We don't do water, fire, or smoke damage repair, mold remediation, or flood cleanup — that's a different trade. We do finishes: the surfaces you see and touch, made to look right again.
